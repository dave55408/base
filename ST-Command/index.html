ST Command controls

The simulation pauses while this window is open. Close it or press Escape to return.

Flight & helm

Left / Right or A / D
Turn the ship or landing craft.
Up / Down
Ship: climb / dive. Landing craft: dive / climb.
Q / E
Climb / dive in either craft.
W / S
Increase / decrease engine power. The game starts at 14% impulse power, with Launch Shuttle available.
Space
All stop.
Mouse controls
Drag the attitude sphere to steer; drag the power dial up or down to set power. Sliders and helm buttons offer the same controls.

Galaxy navigation

Enterprise number pad (space shuttle)
1 / 3 yaw left / right; 5 / 2 pitch like the shuttle Up / Down arrows (dive / climb); 7 / 4 increase / decrease Enterprise power; 8 immediately stops Enterprise; 0 / decimal roll left / right. Hold 1 / 3 to continue banking through any angle, including full rotations; releasing the key holds the bank, and Enterprise keeps turning until leveled. Numpad − smoothly returns yaw, pitch and roll to neutral without changing engine power; an attitude input cancels the return. Hold 4 to brake, then reverse. The tactical inset shows Enterprise attitude and speed. Physical number-pad keys work with Num Lock on or off; shuttle controls remain independent.
Plot & engage
Select a destination or click a star, choose impulse or warp, set power, then press Engage Course. Manual Override releases autopilot.
Drag the galaxy
Orbit the camera above or below the galactic plane.
Scroll or + / minus
Zoom the map. The home icon resets the camera.
Click empty space
Plot a waypoint at the ship's current elevation.

Orbit & landing craft

Clamshell hangar doors
Close automatically when the shuttle is more than 4 km from Enterprise. On return, they reopen automatically within 3 km.
Open / Close Garage
Retract the rear storage door to reveal four Type F shuttles, two across on each of two platform levels, below three observation windows. This door operates independently of the outer clamshell hangar doors.
Enter / leave orbit
Approach within 1 LY of a planet to enter orbit. Increase engine power to orbit faster. Leave Orbit returns to galaxy flight at 2% power; Launch Landing Craft begins a sortie.
Recover the beacon
Select Abandoned beacon and engage for guided descent, or fly manually. Retrieve within 450 m, below 450 m AGL, at 150 m/s or slower.
Return to Enterprise
After retrieval, engage for an aft bay approach. When the bay lip leaves the cockpit view, the camera fades to an external view aligned with your approach angle and roll. Fly inside and press Land Shuttle to descend. Facing Enterprise’s nose lands on the circular pad, then turns the shuttle toward the exit. After turnaround, the view fades back to the fixed launch camera. Choose Power Down to fade to black and return to the bridge, or Launch Again to lift into a hover.
Reverse thrust
Lower engine power below zero with S, the slider, or the power dial. Negative power brakes forward momentum to zero, then moves astern. W increases power; Space / All Stop stops immediately.
Local flight
Warp is unavailable aboard the landing craft. The right map shows terrain or aft bay guidance.
Excursion camera
During a space excursion, press Excursion View for a chase camera following your viewpoint, with the Class F shuttle filling about 20% of screen width. Mouse Look or drag changes the viewing angle; the wheel zooms. Cockpit View or Forward View returns to the shuttle cockpit.
Fly around Enterprise
Enterprise starts at 14% impulse power, so Launch Shuttle is available immediately. Keep engine power below 15% to enable launch. A moving Enterprise continues forward with a gentle port turn while you fly. Shuttle turns produce a slight bank that settles when released. Hold Z or X to roll left or right, including inverted flight; releasing roll holds that attitude. Click the viewscreen or Mouse Look to look around without steering. Scroll to zoom from 0.5× to 8×. Escape releases the mouse; Forward View centers the camera and resets zoom. Engage Course levels the shuttle for a guided aft bay approach. Inside the bay, Land Shuttle lowers the craft to the deck. Facing Enterprise’s nose first centers it on the circular pad and turns it toward the exit after touchdown. Power Down Craft returns to the bridge.

Enterprise model
Choose Second Model or Original Model above the viewscreen. The choice is remembered and can be changed during shuttle flight.
F / double-click viewscreen
Toggle the main viewer fullscreen. Press F, double-click again, or press Escape to return.

Hide / show the shuttle target square, distance label and blue center lines. Guides start off during space excursions.
Enter
Engage a course, or resume when paused.
R
Reset the simulation and mission to Sol at 14% impulse power.
B
Toggle bridge audio. Its audio window provides volume controls.
