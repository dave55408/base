ST Command controls

Start stopped in space on galaxy navigation near Sol at 0% impulse power, with Vulcan selected. Help pauses the simulation; Close or Escape restores your previous pause state.

Flight & helm

Left / Right or A / D
Turn Enterprise or the shuttle.
Up / Down
Enterprise: climb / dive. Shuttle and landing craft: dive / climb.
Q / E
Climb / dive in either craft.
W / S
Increase / decrease power. Below zero, thrust brakes forward motion before reversing.
Z / X (shuttle)
Roll left / right, including inverted flight. Release to hold the roll. The slight bank from turning settles when the turn key is released.
Space / All Stop
Stop the craft you are flying. During a shuttle sortie, use Numpad 8 to stop Enterprise separately.
Mouse helm
Drag the attitude sphere to steer, or drag the power dial up / down to set power. Helm sliders and buttons provide the same controls.

Galaxy navigation

Plot & engage
Select a destination or click a star, choose impulse or warp, set power, then press Engage Course or Enter. Manual Override releases autopilot.
Drag the galaxy
Orbit the map camera above or below the galactic plane.
Scroll or + / minus
Zoom the map. The home icon resets its camera.
Click empty space
Plot a waypoint at Enterprise's current elevation.
Enter / leave orbit
Approach within 1 LY of a planet to enter orbit. Power controls orbital speed. Leave Orbit returns to galaxy navigation at 2% power.
Chase Camera
Show an aft view of Enterprise over the forward viewscreen during galaxy navigation. Forward View restores the normal display.

Shuttle bay & space flight

L: engine-height cockpit
Jump to the shuttle cockpit centered between the engines, halfway from their front to rear and at their center height, facing forward and hovering at zero thrust. Press L again to return to this position during flight. Graphics prepare before the jump.
Launch Shuttle
Use the button for a normal bay launch from galaxy navigation with Enterprise power below 15% in either direction. After graphics preparation, the clamshell doors open and the shuttle lifts from its pad over 1.5 seconds, then hovers until you apply thrust.
Launch camera
The fixed camera stays behind the shuttle during liftoff. As you depart past the second black side door, the view fades into the cockpit.
Find Enterprise
Point the shuttle toward Enterprise and return to cockpit view. This changes direction without engaging autopilot.
Return & land
Engage Course for a guided aft approach, or fly manually. When the bay lip leaves the cockpit view, the camera fades to an aligned external view. Fly inside, slow below 60 m/s, level pitch and roll within 15 degrees, then press Land Shuttle.
Touchdown & relaunch
Facing Enterprise's nose centers the shuttle on the circular pad, lands it, then turns it toward the exit. After the camera returns to the launch view, choose Power Down to return to the bridge, or Launch Again to lift into a hover.
Hangar & garage doors
Open / Close Hangar Door controls the outer clamshells during flight and after landing. Automatic doors close beyond 4 km and reopen within 3 km on return. Open / Close Garage operates the separate rear storage door and reveals four stored shuttles.
Moving Enterprise
Enterprise continues moving after launch if it had power or speed, with a gentle port turn and shallow dive. Stop before launch to keep it stationary, or use the remote helm during flight.

Remote Enterprise helm

Physical number pad
These controls operate Enterprise during a space shuttle sortie, independently of the shuttle. Num Lock may be on or off. The tactical inset shows Enterprise's attitude and speed; its power slider also controls Enterprise.
Numpad 1 / 3
Yaw left / right. Hold to bank through any angle, including full rotations. Release to hold the bank; Enterprise keeps turning until leveled.
Numpad 5 / 2
Dive / climb, matching the shuttle Up / Down arrows.
Numpad 7 / 4
Increase / decrease Enterprise power. Hold 4 to brake and then reverse.
Numpad 8
Immediately stop Enterprise and its automatic drift. Shuttle motion stays independent.
Numpad 0 / decimal
Roll Enterprise left / right. Release to hold attitude.
Numpad minus
Smoothly return yaw, pitch and roll to neutral without changing power. An attitude input cancels the return.

Landing craft & cameras

Launch Landing Craft
Use the button to launch a surface sortie from orbit. Warp is unavailable in local flight; the right map shows terrain or aft bay guidance.
Recover the beacon
Select Abandoned beacon and engage for guided descent, or fly manually. Retrieve within 450 m, below 450 m AGL, at 150 m/s or slower. Then select Enterprise for the return.
Excursion View / Cockpit View
Switch between the exterior shuttle camera and cockpit. Inside the bay, the exterior camera stays fixed while the helm rotates the shuttle.
Mouse Look / drag the viewscreen
Look around without steering during space flight. The wheel zooms from 0.5× to 8×. Escape releases mouse capture. Mouse Look is disabled for the fixed bay camera.
Forward View
Return to the cockpit, center the view and reset zoom.
1 (top row)
Show / hide the target square, distance label and blue center guides. These guides start hidden on space shuttle launches.

Display, performance & simulation

Enterprise model & textures
Choose Original Model, Second Model or Third Model above the viewscreen. Model and texture choices are remembered and can change during flight. Low Memory textures are the default.
Performance
Choose Full, Balanced (75% resolution) or Economy (50%), and a 60 or 30 FPS limit. The panel also shows frame timing and rendering statistics. Settings apply immediately.
Launch preparation
Launch and Launch Again prepare the 3D graphics before liftoff. Wait for Preparing Shuttle to finish; the lift then starts from the pad. Prepared graphics are reused when settings stay the same. Lower resolution reduces rendering work; Low Memory textures reduce memory use.
Night Mode
Dim external lighting to emphasize stars and ship lights. Toggle again to restore normal lighting; the choice is remembered.
H
Open / close the aft bridge hatch to view its Y-shaped room and bridge picture.
F / double-click viewscreen
Toggle the main viewer fullscreen. F, another double-click or Escape returns.
P / Enter
P pauses or resumes. Enter resumes when paused; otherwise it engages a course.
R
Reset to galaxy navigation at Sol, stopped with 0% impulse power.
B
Toggle bridge audio. Open its audio window to adjust volume.
Project notes
Save notes in project-notes.html. When opening the game directly from your computer, choose the existing HTML file or use Save Notes to create it. Browser drafts remain until saved.
