ST Command controls

This is an open-ended simulator for folks who love the classic USS Enterprise.

Hint: For a quickstart, press "L" for "Look" to jump to flying the shuttle: Arrows keys = up/down/left/right, "W" and "S" for forward/backward. "A" and "D" for roll.

Things to do:

  • Launch or land a shuttle
  • Fly around the Enterprise
  • Check out the Bridge (press "B")
  • Peek in the shuttle garage
  • Visit Vulcan (not fully done)
  • Night Mode

Neutral returns yaw, pitch and roll without changing power; manual attitude input cancels it. Enterprise's Home stop also clears held remote controls. Shuttle and remote Enterprise power stay independent.

Start stopped in space on galaxy navigation near Sol at 0% impulse power, with Vulcan selected. Help pauses the simulation; Close or Escape restores your previous pause state.

Galaxy navigation

Mouse helm
Drag the attitude sphere to steer, or drag the power dial up / down to adjust power. Helm sliders and buttons provide the same controls.
Plot & engage
Select a destination or click a star, choose impulse or warp, set power, then press Engage Course. Manual Override releases autopilot.
Drag the galaxy
Orbit the map camera above or below the galactic plane.
Scroll or + / minus
Zoom the map. The home icon resets its camera.
Click empty space
Plot a waypoint at Enterprise's current elevation.
Enter / leave orbit
Approach within 1 LY of a planet to enter orbit. Power controls orbital speed. Leave Orbit returns to galaxy navigation at 2% power.
Chase Camera
Show an aft view of Enterprise over the forward viewscreen during galaxy navigation. Forward View restores the normal display.

Shuttle bay & space flight

Launch Shuttle
Use the button for a normal bay launch from galaxy navigation with Enterprise power below 15% in either direction. After graphics preparation, the clamshell doors open and the shuttle lifts from its pad over 1.5 seconds, then hovers until you apply thrust.
Launch camera
The fixed camera stays behind the shuttle during liftoff. As you depart past the second black side door, the view fades into the cockpit.
Find Enterprise
Point the shuttle toward Enterprise and return to cockpit view. This changes direction without engaging autopilot.
Track Enterprise
Film Enterprise from the shuttle with smooth, stabilized panning and some space ahead of its apparent motion. Both ships' movement affects the framing. Tracking preserves your zoom, flight path and power. Toggle off, drag the view, or choose Forward View, Excursion View or Find Enterprise to stop tracking.
Return & land
Engage Course for a guided aft approach, or fly manually. When the bay lip leaves the cockpit view, the camera fades to an aligned external view. Fly inside, slow below 60 m/s, level pitch and roll within 15 degrees, then press Land Shuttle.
Touchdown & relaunch
Facing Enterprise's nose centers the shuttle on the circular pad, lands it, then turns it toward the exit. After the camera returns to the launch view, choose Power Down to return to the bridge, or Launch Again to lift into a hover.
Hangar & garage doors
Open / Close Hangar Door controls the outer clamshells during flight and after landing. One minute after the shuttle clears the bay, the hangar and garage doors close automatically. Returning inside cancels the countdown; pausing also pauses it. Flying nearby does not open the hangar; use Open Hangar Door when returning. Launch and landing sequences can open it. Open / Close Garage operates the separate rear storage door and reveals four stored shuttles.
Moving Enterprise
Enterprise continues moving after launch if it had power or speed, with a gentle port turn and shallow dive. Stop before launch to keep it stationary, or use the remote helm during flight.

Landing craft & cameras

Launch Landing Craft
Use the button to launch a surface sortie from orbit. Warp is unavailable in local flight; the right map shows terrain or aft bay guidance.
Recover the beacon
Select Abandoned beacon and engage for guided descent, or fly manually. Retrieve within 450 m, below 450 m AGL, at 150 m/s or slower. Then select Enterprise for the return.
Excursion View / Cockpit View
Switch between the exterior shuttle camera and cockpit. Inside the bay, the exterior camera stays fixed while the helm rotates the shuttle.
Drag the viewscreen
Look around without steering during space flight. The wheel zooms from 0.5× to 8×. Dragging is disabled for the fixed bay camera.
Forward View
Return to the cockpit, center the view and reset zoom.
1 (top row)
Show / hide the target square, distance label and blue center guides. These guides start hidden on space shuttle launches.

Display, performance & simulation

Enterprise & textures
Enterprise uses the Third Model. Choose Low Memory or High Detail textures above the viewscreen; texture choices are remembered and can change during flight. Low Memory is the default.
Performance
Choose Full, Balanced (75% resolution) or Economy (50%), and a 60 or 30 FPS limit. The panel also shows frame timing and rendering statistics. Settings apply immediately.
Launch preparation
Launch and Launch Again prepare the 3D graphics before liftoff. A transparent Preparing Shuttle message appears on the main viewscreen while loading. Wait for preparation to finish; the lift then starts from the pad. Prepared graphics are reused when settings stay the same. Lower resolution reduces rendering work; Low Memory textures reduce memory use.
Night Mode
Dim external lighting to emphasize stars and ship lights. Toggle again to restore normal lighting; the choice is remembered.
H
Open / close the aft bridge hatch. The red split door at the bridge end of the corridor starts opening one second after the outer hatch. On closing, the red inner door shuts first, then the outer hatch starts closing after a two-second wait.
F / double-click viewscreen
Toggle the main viewer fullscreen. F, another double-click or Escape returns.
R
Reset to galaxy navigation at Sol, stopped with 0% impulse power.
B
Go to the bridge, facing the viewscreen.
Shift+B
Toggle bridge audio. Open its audio window to adjust volume.
Version
The toolbar follows Semantic Versioning 2.0.0: MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH. Major versions indicate incompatible changes, minor versions add compatible features, and patch versions fix bugs. Build shows the automatically updated build ID alongside Version.
