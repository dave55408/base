ST Command controls

The simulation pauses while this window is open. Close it or press Escape to return.

Flight & helm

Left / Right or A / D
Turn the ship or landing craft.
Up / Down
Ship: climb / dive. Landing craft: dive / climb.
Q / E
Climb / dive in either craft.
W / S
Increase / decrease engine power.
Space
All stop.
Mouse controls
Drag the attitude sphere to steer; drag the power dial up or down to set power. Sliders and helm buttons offer the same controls.

Galaxy navigation

Plot & engage
Select a destination or click a star, choose impulse or warp, set power, then press Engage Course. Manual Override releases autopilot.
Drag the galaxy
Orbit the camera above or below the galactic plane.
Scroll or + / minus
Zoom the map. The home icon resets the camera.
Click empty space
Plot a waypoint at the ship's current elevation.

Orbit & landing craft

Enter / leave orbit
Approach within 1 LY of a planet to enter orbit. Leave Orbit returns to galaxy flight; Launch Landing Craft begins a sortie.
Recover the beacon
Select Abandoned beacon and engage for guided descent, or fly manually. Retrieve within 450 m, below 450 m AGL, at 150 m/s or slower.
Return to Enterprise
After retrieval, engage to approach the aft bay. Land within 650 m, aligned from aft at heading 000° ±15°, pitch ±12°, and 120 m/s or slower. Power Down Craft returns to the bridge.
Local flight
Warp is unavailable aboard the landing craft. The right map shows terrain or aft bay guidance.

View & simulation

Double-click viewscreen
Expand to full screen; double-click again or press Escape to return.
P
Pause / resume.
Enter
Engage a course, or resume when paused.
R
Reset the simulation and mission.
B
Toggle bridge audio. Its audio window provides volume controls.
