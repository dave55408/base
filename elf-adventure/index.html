Game behavior

Bring gifts to the elves' cottages to earn their love and jewels. The game pauses while this window is open.

Goal and controls

Collect Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, Amethyst, Moonstone, Nightstone, and Topaz. Drop all seven in the possessions area inside GOLDEN KEEP TREASURY to win. Jewels you are carrying, or that the bat is carrying, do not count as stored.

Use the arrow keys or joystick to move. Touch an item to pick it up; press Space or BUTTON to drop or use what you carry. Ring bells with empty hands. Press Tab to show or hide the map and hints, and P to pause. New quests start with the map and hints visible.

A successful bell call becomes a Happy, Loving elf's next priority. It puts down other carried items and pauses gift reviews and retrievals until helping ends, then resumes unfinished gift work. When an eligible friend hears a call, the bell's outer border immediately starts a two-second color cycle, repeating every four seconds until the elf reaches its helping position. The bell's interior stays brown. Its border returns to brown between cycles and after arrival. If no friend answers, it stays brown.

Emotions and love

Elves start Neutral. Mood ranges from Sad (1) to Happy (3); Sentiment ranges from Loves (1) to Hate (3). Use Show emotions to see their current feelings.

A liked gift raises Mood by one when the elf finishes placing it. A hated gift lowers Mood on pickup. Neutral items do not change Mood. Two liked gifts earn Loves; hated and neutral gifts do not count. New or moved items anywhere inside an elf's cottage send it home for review. It stops roaming, attacking, or retrieving items to review waiting gifts; bell help takes priority, so reviews wait until helping ends; carried items are put down first. An elf already handling a cottage gift finishes that task before reviewing the next one.

Elves keep one of each liked item in their treasures. They carry hated items to a nearby room outside their home area. Belthir carries hated flutes to Willow Crossing; the flute plays on a loop until he puts it down. Extra liked items go to The Crossroads and still count as liked gifts. Three plants appear in random outdoor locations each quest. All elves like these flowers; they raise Mood and count toward earning Loves.

Roaming and movement

A Happy, Loving elf can fetch an item you dropped or swapped away in the same outdoor room, then bring it near you for pickup. Move at least 100 pixels away to request help. Elves finish cottage gift tasks first. They leave stored treasures, axes, bridges, and the bat alone. If you leave the room or the friendship sours, they put down the item.

Elves roam between rooms but do not forage for loose gifts. Bring items inside their cottages for assessment. Owners can use their own front doors even when their cottage gate is closed; you still need the matching key. They step away from doorways and walk around obstacles. If you block an elf's route to a gift, it pauses briefly before trying again; moving away lets it continue immediately.

Moonwood forest gate quest

Amethyst rests inside White Castle, guarded by Rhindle. Go south from Moonwell Vault through Moonwood Trail to Moonwood Grove, then east to Moonwood Shrine for Moonstone.

The bell and wooden switch are on the left side of Moonwood Shrine. Walk over the switch to press it and raise the vine gate, even while carrying an item. Its handle moves immediately. Stay on the switch to hold it down; it springs back when you walk away, and the gate closes before you can reach the shrine. Make an elf Happy and earn its Loves sentiment with liked gifts, and return any stolen property, then press Space or RING BELL beside the brass bell with empty hands. Stay in this room while your friend pauses its current task and walks to the switch. Your friend holds the gate while you retrieve Moonstone, until you leave the room, the elf is stunned, or the elf is no longer Happy or no longer Loves you. The bat and magnet cannot take Moonstone from its forest seal.

Midnight Vault and Umbra's cache

Go south from Sapphire Shore through Duskwood Trail, a woodland room with five trees. A path covers the southern 30% of the room and leads to Lamplight Landing. Its bell is just to the right of the bottom doorway, which leads south into Midnight Vault. Black Nightstone and three coins lie in Umbra's cache in the lower-right corner.

Without the lamp, the vault is pitch black, its walls remain visible, and Umbra stays still. The lower-right cache area is blocked in the dark. Bringing the lamp into the vault reveals about 98% of the room when centered, with a 16-pixel stepped light edge, opens access to the cache, and immediately turns Umbra's eye orange. If you carry or leave the lamp there yourself, Umbra swiftly attacks you.

To retrieve Nightstone, earn a Happy, Loving elf's friendship and return any possessions you stole from that elf. Leave the lamp on the ground, then ring Lamplight Landing's bell with empty hands. A Happy, Loving friend pauses its current task and collects it as its next priority. Stay in Lamplight Landing or Midnight Vault while your friend comes.

Your friend carries the lamp into Midnight Vault and stands in the upper-left corner. Umbra follows the light and stares at it, but still snaps if you cross his mouth. Move away quickly to escape the bite. Once your friend is in position and Umbra is staring, you can collect Nightstone. The jewel requires your friend's help: the sword, axe, bat, and magnet cannot bypass its seal.

When you leave Midnight Vault, Umbra returns to guard the cache and your friend brings the lamp back to Lamplight Landing. Ring the bell again if you need another visit. If the helper loses happiness or Loves, is stunned, or has a surprised reaction, it puts down the lamp and stops helping.

Jewels and possessions

Black Castle's gate starts raised, but still works with the black key. Bring the key outside to operate it like the other castle gates. Its left tower has crumbled. Topaz lies just above and to the left of the black key in the Black Dungeon beyond Black Castle Hall; walk over it to collect it.

Elves remember which possessions you steal. Having two stolen possessions from the same elf turns its Sentiment to Hate, causing it to pursue and attack you with axes. Return an item anywhere inside its owner's cottage to repair that theft's Mood penalty. Returning property does not automatically remove Hate and is not a new gift; the same object can earn gift credit only once per elf. Liked gifts can restore Loves. An elf with an outstanding theft will not retrieve items for you.

Each elf's home key starts in the first treasure slot at the top left. Its jewel starts below the far-right treasure column. House gates open with their matching key and slide upward like Golden Keep's gate.

An elf with the Loves sentiment offers its jewel in the gift area if it has received two liked gifts or is Happy. It pauses for one second before collecting the jewel to share it. If Loves is lost, it tries to return the jewel to its protected corner while the jewel remains inside its cottage. Removing a possession from the possessions area lowers Mood by one.

A jewel in the protected corner has a circular barrier centered on it. The circle matches the width of a treasure slot. Its protection stops while the owner is knocked out, after the initial surprised reaction ends. Protection returns when the owner recovers. A shared jewel outside the protected corner can be collected without knocking out the elf.

Elf movement and axes

All three elves walk at the same speed in every emotional state. They roam, assess gifts, retrieve items for friends, hold the forest switch, and carry the lamp. They place returned items clear of their feet and can step away from items that overlap them. A Sad, Hateful elf puts down any non-axe item, finds an axe, pursues you between rooms, and throws it at you. Elves with Hate also attack at other Mood levels. An axe hit stuns you for five seconds and drops your carried item. Your cursor shakes slightly while stunned. An available angry elf within 200 pixels tries to steal the dropped item and take it home. Giving liked gifts can restore Loves and stop attacks; elves finish cottage gift tasks before hunting.

Elves do not throw more axes while you are stunned. They can resume attacking after you recover. Thrown axes stay where they land. You can pick them up and throw them again.

An axe hit makes an elf Sad and Hateful: it looks surprised for 0.5 seconds, then stays knocked out for 15 seconds. After recovering, a Hateful elf looks for an axe and attacks. Each elf starts with an axe in its possessions area. Two more axes start in Golden Keep and Western Maze's Root Gallery.

Dragons

The Debug section's Dragons toggle starts on Easy. Ordinary dragons have a 0.65-second bite delay on Easy and a 0.4-second delay on Hard. Hard makes all dragons move 50% faster. Switching takes effect immediately, including during a bite, and the selection stays in effect through quest restarts until the page is refreshed.

Ordinary dragons chase you in the same room and can follow you between rooms, but cannot follow you into Midnight Vault. Carrying the sword makes them flee; touching one with the sword slays it. Umbra guards Midnight Vault, ignores the sword, and cannot be slain. Its bite takes 0.25 seconds on Easy and about 0.15 seconds on Hard. Umbra keeps pursuing during its lunge, so running past it can still get you eaten. Being swallowed ends the game.

An axe stuns a living dragon for two seconds without killing it, freezing both movement and its bite. Its eye shows the knocked-out state while stunned. Umbra still requires a friend's lamp distraction to release Nightstone.

The bat

The bat starts in Wyrmwood Wilds and flies between rooms. It can carry objects and ordinary dragons, steal what you hold, or swap its carried object for yours. It cannot carry Umbra or take a sealed Moonstone or Nightstone. Touching it with empty hands picks it up. With hints enabled, a small w tracks it on the map.
