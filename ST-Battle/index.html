        01 / CHOOSE A MANEUVER

        Choose direct, port/starboard curve, above, below, attack from behind, K7 cover then assault, keep your distance, ram, close battle, chase, or flee and repair for up to three slots. Delete removes a selected maneuver. Automatic helm executes selected maneuvers in order. Choose any combination of weapon orders; phasers use 28% capacitor per shot and each ship carries 24 torpedoes per battle; all selected maneuvers share approximately 30 seconds, including repairs, which use available parts.

        02 / GUARD YOUR WEAK SIDE

        Route shield recharge to six sectors. “To threat” reinforces the side facing Yorktown. Choose an above, below or curving maneuver to change the engagement geometry.

        03 / MAKE THE SHOT

        Select a shot tab, choose its weapon card, rotate Yorktown in 3D and click a target marker or label, then set the range slider and Save. Light up the maneuver buttons for each attack. Defaults are M1, M2 and M3; all attacks can share one maneuver and each fires once; additional manual shots use energy and ammunition. Save all three attacks, then Commit. The green range band gives +15% damage: phasers 5?10 km, torpedoes 3?7 km. Shots execute in sequence when within range and arc. The Phaser button and T fire additional manual shots without changing scheduled orders. Two revisions are available for pending actions. Edits remain staged until Apply changes; Cancel, Escape or close discards them. Selected shields must face you.

        04 / BALANCE THE SHIP

        Power shares always total 100%. More weapons means less for other stations. Damaged sensors lose telemetry and locks; damaged reactors lose usable output. C scans. Repair teams use limited parts. Bridge damage reduces all output; main-engine damage further limits propulsion. Primary-hull orders increase hull damage. Planning and review freeze motion, repairs and recharge. The 30-second assessment clock waits for Commit. Your maneuver plan shares a 30-second execution window: about 30 seconds for one maneuver, 15 each for two, or 10 each for three. Projectiles and breakaway finish afterward. Two phasers hitting the same shield open a six-second torpedo window for 50% extra system damage. P pauses the simulation too.

        Personnel files

        Enterprise / Command

        Captain Declan

        A former rescue commander, Declan earned his chair bringing stranded crews home from the frontier. He favors patient positioning over glorious charges, but commits completely when an opening appears. His dry humor steadies the bridge; his private rule is simple: never spend a life to save his pride.

        Enterprise / Helm

        Lieutenant Erica

        Erica learned to navigate among the crowded orbital yards of her home colony. Exacting, observant, and almost impossible to rattle, she can read a closing angle before the sensors finish their sweep. Her calm reports hide a fierce protective streak: she always knows the nearest way out.

        Enterprise / Flight control / Vulcan

        Lieutenant Allison

        A Vulcan shuttle test pilot, Allison approaches three-dimensional flight with exacting logic and exceptional spatial discipline. Her measured reports and precise course corrections steady the bridge under pressure. Off duty she studies antique flight simulators as exercises in probability; on duty she trusts Erica's judgment and regards unnecessary risk as an error in calculation.

        Yorktown / Opposing command

        Captain Geffen

        Once a respected tactical instructor, Geffen knows how captains are taught to fight?and where that training makes them predictable. He prefers to pressure a weak shield, force a hurried decision, then close for the finish. Behind his cool manner lies a dangerous flaw: he hates conceding the initiative.
